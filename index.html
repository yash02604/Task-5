Golden Triangle Tour

Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights

Journey Overview

Designed for travelers looking for a seamlessly managed private journey through India's Golden Triangle. Travel in air-conditioned comfort with a dedicated chauffeur and private city guides.

Highlights

  • Sunrise guided viewing of the Taj Mahal in Agra.
  • Private Elephant or Jeep ride to Amber Fort, Jaipur.
  • Full day Old & New Delhi heritage tour (Jama Masjid, Humayun Tomb).
  • Dedicated luxury sedan or SUV (Toyota Innova Crysta).

Inclusions & Support

Includes private AC vehicle, bottled water, driver allowances, toll taxes, state taxes, fuel, and licensed local English-speaking city tour guides.
